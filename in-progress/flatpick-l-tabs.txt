Bonaparte's Retreat

Traditional

Tab by Bo Parker

This is an excerpt from "Bonaparte's Retreat" as played by fiddler William Stepp, and recorded by Alan Lomax. This then was used by Aaron Copland in the music to the ballet "Rodeo." FWIW, it's now best known as the theme tune for a beef commercial.

There was an article on this on NPR's "All Things Considered" this week, and this inspired me to work out this little snippet, which sits pretty well on guitar and is very recognizable. It may not be exactly right, but it's close.

Banks of the Ohio

Traditional

Tab and Notes by Rob Bradley

Here's the TAB for a solo to a familiar beginners' tune: The Banks of the Ohio. The melody itself is very well known, so I haven't included it. This solo counts as simple crosspicking, and so I guess it would be considered intermediate flatpicking.

Some notes: fingering is indicated in measures 4-5; play the G7 by barring the 3rd fret with index finger across strings 2-4 (or even 1-4). This is the only thing in the whole solo which is even a little challenging. In measures 7 and 15 (but NOT 13), I syncopate the Lester Flatt run by shortening the quarter note and lengthening the eighth note that follows. I don't go as far as an eighth-quarter-eighth rhythm, though. A good way to get it right is to pick the first three notes down-up-up, but to think of them in the written rhythm.
